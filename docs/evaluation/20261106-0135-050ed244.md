Measured hours: `queued_hours` 23.038 (`slot_hours.multi_ready_waiting`), 0.561 of the 41.092 live wall hours. P1 raises the bound to 4, not higher, because one driver process runs at most four stages at once (`MAX_CONCURRENCY`, crates/rigger-conductor/src/conductor.rs:77): `admissible` refuses a fifth running stage before it reads the width (conductor.rs:15444), so a wider bound lets only parked units hold extra slots. The baseline's `units_in_flight_max` of 9 is the maximum over its 14 earlier runs, not a figure under today's admission code. At 4 a fan-out of more than four ready units still queues, so the 23.038 h P1 claims is the measured ceiling the check allows, not what it will remove; the next file measures the part, and any part above 1.545 h keeps it first. The radius rule did not hold a unit back and does not under today's code: `radii_recorded_before_start` is 0 because a planner-proposed unit's stage carries no `partition` (the stage `harvest_proposed` builds at conductor.rs:13282 sets none, and `defaults` declares none), so `wave_radii` returns no radii (conductor.rs:5105) and the width bound alone admits.
